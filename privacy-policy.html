Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

We collect nothing. No personal data, no analytics, no tracking. Your study progress saves locally on your device and never leaves it. Full stop.

Information We Collect

Paralegal Exam Canada does not collect any personal information whatsoever. This includes:

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No account registration or sign-in
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reports sent to any server
  • No cookies or cross-site tracking

How Your Data is Stored

All study data — your XP points, exam scores, bookmarks, streaks, settings, and province preferences — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never shared with any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time via Settings → Reset All Progress
  • Is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app

Third-Party Services

Paralegal Exam Canada uses no third-party analytics services (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Mixpanel), no advertising networks, and no social media tracking pixels.

Children's Privacy

This application is designed for adults pursuing professional paralegal licensing and legal knowledge. It is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

Question Accuracy Disclaimer

The 565 practice questions in this application are AI-generated study material. While every effort has been made to ensure accuracy, questions may not reflect the most current exam content, case law, or regulatory changes. Always verify exam preparation with official law society resources. Paralegal Exam Canada is not affiliated with the Law Society of Ontario, any provincial or territorial law society, or any other regulatory body.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since we collect no data, any changes would relate only to how we describe the app's non-data-collection practices.
