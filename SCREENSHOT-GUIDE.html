📸 App Store Screenshot Guide

iPhone 14 Pro Max · 2796 × 1290 px · iOS App Store

Device: iPhone 14 Pro Max
Resolution: 2796 × 1290 pixels (6.7" display)
Required by: App Store Connect — 6.7" Screenshots
Your device: ✅ Exact match — use your real phone!
Format: PNG or JPEG, no transparency
Min needed: 3 screenshots (5–8 recommended)
Before you start: Your phone's screenshots are already the perfect resolution for the App Store. No resizing, no tools needed — just take them directly from your iPhone 14 Pro Max and upload as-is.

Step 1 — Load Demo Data

1 Open the app and go to Settings ⚙️
Tap the gear icon in the top right corner of the Home screen. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings sheet to find the "About & Legal" section.
2 Tap "📸 Load Demo Data for Screenshots"
This populates the app with realistic data: 1,250 XP (Law Clerk rank), 213 questions answered, a 12-day streak, 84% best exam, upcoming exam date, and sample bookmarks and missed questions. Tap OK when prompted.
3 The app will reload — you're ready to shoot
The Home screen now looks like an active user's app. You'll see "Law Clerk" as your rank, filled-in stats, a streak, and 47 days to exam.

Step 2 — Take Screenshots

Take at least 5 screenshots in this order. For each: navigate to the screen, make sure it looks right, then press Side Button + Volume Up simultaneously to capture.

1

Home Screen — Your Stats REQUIRED

The main home screen showing "Law Clerk" rank, XP, streak, accuracy, and the exam countdown. This is your hero screenshot — the first thing potential users see. Make sure the hero card is fully visible. Scroll up if needed so the green card with "Law Clerk" shows at the top.

2

A Question In Progress REQUIRED

Tap "Start Exam" → Quick (15 questions). Answer the first question — pick an answer and tap it so it shows highlighted. Screenshot shows the clean question UI with options, timer, and progress indicator. Ideally don't submit yet — just have one answer selected.

3

Exam Results — Passing Score REQUIRED

Complete a Quick exam (15 questions). Aim for 70%+ to show a "Pass" result. The results screen shows the score ring, grade, correct/total, and breakdown by area. This demonstrates the depth of feedback the app provides.

4

Province Selector RECOMMENDED

Go to Settings → Change Province. The province selector grid shows all 13 provinces/territories. This communicates the pan-Canadian scope immediately. The "Full" and "Core" badges should be visible.

5

Language Picker — 35 Languages RECOMMENDED

Go to Settings → tap the Language button. The 3-column grid of 35 flags is visually striking and communicates international reach. Screenshot the full grid — scroll up so the title "Select Language" is visible.

6

Drills & Study Tools OPTIONAL

Scroll down on the Home screen to show the Drills section — Quick Mix, Missed Questions (5 saved from demo data), Bookmarked (6 bookmarks), Flashcards, Topic Drills. Shows the breadth of study modes.

7

Flashcard in Action OPTIONAL

Tap Flashcards from the Home screen. Show a card before or after the flip. The clean two-sided format works well as a screenshot.

Step 3 — Upload to App Store Connect

1 Find screenshots in Photos app
All screenshots save to your Camera Roll automatically. In the Photos app, look under Recents or Screenshots album.
2 AirDrop to your Mac
Select all screenshots in Photos → Share → AirDrop → select your Mac. They'll land in your Downloads folder as PNG files.
3 Upload in App Store Connect
Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com → your app → iOS App → 6.7" Display section. Drag and drop your PNG files. App Store Connect accepts iPhone 14 Pro Max screenshots for the 6.7" display requirement with no conversion needed.

Caption Ideas for Each Screenshot

App Store Connect lets you add 3-line captions that appear over screenshots in some storefronts. Use these:

  • Screenshot 1: "Master Canadian Law · Track Your Progress · Build Your Rank"
  • Screenshot 2: "565 Practice Questions · All 13 Provinces · Timed Exams"
  • Screenshot 3: "Detailed Score Breakdown · Know Your Weaknesses · Improve Fast"
  • Screenshot 4: "Every Province & Territory · Province-Specific Question Banks"
  • Screenshot 5: "35 Languages · Study in Your Language · Global Reach"

After Screenshots — Reset to Fresh

Remember: After taking screenshots, go to Settings → Reset All Progress to clear the demo data before submitting the app for review. App Review uses a clean device and should start with a fresh app state.
